When did shawarma first appear in Kenya ?

Shawarma first appeared in Kenya during the late 1990s and early 2000s, introduced by Middle Eastern immigrants—primarily from Lebanon and Syria—who opened authentic eateries in Nairobi and Mombasa. Originally a niche dish for expatriates, it rapidly broke into the mainstream fast-food scene in the 2010s through shopping malls and local franchises. Today, shawarma has evolved into a ubiquitous Kenyan street food staple, often customized with local spice blends, vibrant chili sauces, and french fries. ( Shawarmaz's Global History Feature.)
